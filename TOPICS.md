# EEEN20020 Electrical and Electronic Circuits: Topic Tracker (final exam)

**Exam:** Tuesday 15 December. **2 hours. 3 questions, answer all: Q1 is 100 marks (5 parts of 20%), Q2 and Q3 are 50 marks each.** Same layout in all ten past papers. 65% of the module.

**Open book.** Bring any printed notes, but no laptop or phone. A calculator is allowed. Print the [exam sheet](formula-sheet.md) and bring it.

**Plan:** [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) (day by day to Tue 15 Dec, light around your other exams).

**What each question is, every year:**
- **Q1:** five short calculations: a resistive network (Topic 5), a transient (Topic 9), an impedance (Topic 11), an AC power or transformer part (Topics 12, 13), and an op-amp filter or a diode (Topics 15, 16, 17).
- **Q2:** frequency response: H(ω), a special frequency, Vout(t) (Topics 14, 15).
- **Q3:** usually a diode waveform: sketch, max, min, period (Topic 17). Sometimes a transistor amplifier (Topic 6), a transformer load (Topic 13) or an RC square wave (Topic 9).

**Weight** = how often it's in the ten past papers. ●●● = must know · ●● = should know · ● = skim · ✗ = skip

Mark progress by changing `☐` to `✅`. (Only ticked when you say a topic is done.)
