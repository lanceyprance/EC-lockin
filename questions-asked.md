## Slips found in the course material

These aren't questions you asked. They're mistakes in the slides or papers worth knowing about.

- **Lecture 22 notes** are headed "Lecture 23". The content is Lecture 22 (transformer and resonance).
- **Lecture 8 notes** are marked "not updated". Where they differ from the Lecture 8 slides (revision 3), trust the slides.
- **Final 2025 Q1e:** the official answer calls 8.6 V the "peak-peak value of V56". With no load the capacitor holds a steady 8.6 V, so it's really the peak (dc) value. 📍 [Topic 17](notes/17-rectifiers-clippers.md)
- **Final 2024 Q3(c):** no number is given for the turns ratio N1:N2. Resit 2025 Q3 uses 10:1. 📍 [Topic 13](notes/13-transformer-resonance.md)
- **Final 2023 Q2(c), (d):** no component values are given. Answer with formulas. 📍 [Topic 15](notes/15-op-amp-filters.md)
- **Final 2024 Q1e:** the question says VA = 4.5 V but the answer box says 5 V. Either way e3 = 5 V. 📍 [Topic 16](notes/16-diodes.md)
- **Resit 2022 Q1a:** the question asks for the power in RL, the answer box says Rs. Both are 125 μW (matched). 📍 [Topic 13](notes/13-transformer-resonance.md)
- **Final 2024 Q1b:** the arrow for I points against the real current, so as drawn I is negative. 📍 [Topic 9](notes/09-transients.md)
