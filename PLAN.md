# Study plan: EEEN20020 final, Tuesday 15 December

20 study days (Wed 25 Nov to Mon 14 Dec). You didn't give study hours, so this assumes about **2.5 hours on weekdays and 4 hours at weekends**, more in the last week once your other exams are done: roughly **55 hours** in total. If your real hours are different, say so and the plan gets redone.

**Your other exams:**
- **MATH20290, Sat 5 Dec.** EC is light Thu 3 to Sat 5.
- **DE (EEEN20050), Tue 8 Dec.** EC is light Sun 6 to Tue 8.
- **SSD (EEEN20070), Wed 16 Dec: the day after EC.** So keep Mon 14 light, and don't plan anything for the evening of the 15th except SSD.

**For every topic:** read the notes and answer each 🧠 check on paper. Then do the end-of-topic test **with your notes open, like the real exam**, but try each question before you look anything up. Mark each question ✓ ~ ✗ (on the site, or on paper). Anything ✗ or ~ goes in the 🔁 Relearn list in [TOPICS.md](TOPICS.md).

Tick each day off by changing `☐` to `✅`.
